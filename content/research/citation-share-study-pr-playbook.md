# Citation-Share Study: PR Angles & Wave Plan (Internal)

Right. The methods page does the credibility work. This does the coverage work. Read it once, then execute — the value of a data study is 20% the data and 80% getting it in front of the people who repeat numbers for a living.

One rule before anything else: **nothing here gets pitched with a real figure until wave 1 closes.** The angles are templates. We fill the blank, then we pitch. Pitching a number we haven't produced is how a data study loses the only thing it has — trust.

---

## The five headline angles

The format is the angle. The number is whatever the data says. We do not decide the number in advance — we decide the shape of the story, and the shape holds regardless of which way the data breaks.

| # | Angle | Headline template | Best target tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The flagship | "The median site appears in only X% of AI answers about its own category" | Trade |
| 2 | Vertical league table | "[Vertical] sites get cited N× more than [vertical] in AI answers" | Newsletter |
| 3 | The mention-without-link economy | "Y% of AI citations name the source but never link it" | Newsletter |
| 4 | Engine divergence | "ChatGPT and Perplexity agree on which sites to cite only Z% of the time" | Trade |
| 5 | The quarterly delta | "AI citation share moved N points in 90 days" | Mainstream / recurring |

Angle 1 is the launch story — it inherits the pilot's 8% and asks whether it holds at scale. Angle 5 is the engine that keeps the study earning coverage: every wave produces a fresh delta, and a delta is inherently newsworthy in a way a static benchmark stops being after the first telling. That recurring delta is the reason the cadence in the methods doc is quarterly and not annual. Cadence is a distribution decision as much as a measurement one.

## Asset kit — ship one per wave

No pitch goes out without all four. The kit is the deliverable; the email is just the cover note.

- [ ] **One headline chart** — median citation share by vertical (the league table). This is the shareable artefact.
- [ ] **One shareable table** — the same data, clean, copy-pasteable into a post or slide.
- [ ] **Aggregate CSV** — the download, linked from the methods page. This is the "you can check my work" asset.
- [ ] **150-word canned summary** — written so a journalist can lift it verbatim. If they have to rewrite it, some won't run it.

## Wave-1 plan (team: Sunny + 1)

Methods-first sequencing. We publish the methodology page *before* the findings — so journalists can pre-verify the instrument, and so the number arrives with its receipts already public. That order is itself a differentiator; incumbents publish findings and let you take the method on faith.

| Week | Work | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Freeze the site frame + prompt grammar; build the prompt registry | Assistant builds; Sunny signs off vertical seeds |
| 2–3 | Field wave 1 — 16,000 prompts × 3 runs (~30 automated hours + 2 days manual QA on a 5% sample) | Assistant |
| 4 | Scoring + QA; **publish the methodology page** (the public methods doc) | Assistant |
| 5 | Findings post + PR kit + pitches | Sunny pitches trade tier personally; assistant handles newsletter tier |
| Recurring | 1 week fielding + 1 week publish, per quarter | Split as above |

Notes for the wave:

- Publish the methods page a clear beat ahead of the findings post. Let it get indexed and, ideally, get one trade pre-read before the number lands.
- Sunny takes trade personally — Danny and Barry are relationships, not a mail-merge. Newsletter tier is a clean templated send with the chart and CSV.
- Mainstream (Exploding Topics, Axios) only gets pitched once at least one trade outlet has run it. Borrowed credibility travels; a cold mainstream pitch on a marketing-site study usually doesn't.
- The digital-PR mechanics — how to actually land these — live under [Digital PR for Citations](/distribution/digital-pr/). This file is what to pitch; that page is how.

Execute the newsletter and mainstream sends within 72 hours of the findings post going live. No improvising the angle mid-wave — the templates are the angle, on purpose.
