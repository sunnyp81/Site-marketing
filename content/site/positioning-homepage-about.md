## PART 0 — POSITIONING STATEMENT

> **site.marketing is the operator's manual for websites that already exist. One sequence — measure, fix, strengthen, amplify — instead of the twenty-one channels you'll never actually finish.**

*(25 words. This is the brand promise. Everything below enforces it.)*

Three positions are baked in and non-negotiable:

1. **Existing sites only.** Zero "start a blog" content. Ever. Website creation is a different entity and we refuse it on purpose.
2. **Sequence, not channels.** There is a correct order. Amplifying a leaky site is paying to lose faster.
3. **SEO and GEO are one discipline, not two retainers.** Anyone billing them separately is billing the same work twice.

# Marketing for Websites That Already Exist

### 1.1 Hero

Every guide to marketing a website was written for someone who launched yesterday — 21 channels, pick a few, good luck. You didn't launch yesterday. You've got history, indexed pages, and a traffic line that stopped climbing a while ago.

This is the other kind of guide. It assumes your site exists and gets to work.

> **The median established site shows up in 8% of relevant AI answers.** Yours is probably sitting at 8% too. That's fixable — in a specific order.

### 1.2 Proof

No logo wall. Three numbers instead.

| 8% | 14 years | 4 steps |
|---|---|---|
| Median AI citation share across our 50-site, 20-prompt audit. Original data nobody else has. | Sunny's time in SEO. Long enough to have deleted things on purpose. | The entire method. Not twenty-one. Four. |

I've deleted 400 pages from a site and watched traffic go up. Clients love that conversation, right up until they see the graph. I'm Sunny, by the way — [more on the About page](/about/).

No testimonials here yet. The site is new, and we don't invent quotes.

### 1.3 The Sequence: Measure → Fix → Strengthen → Amplify

There is a correct order, and this is it. Do it out of order and you spend money faster than you make it.

1. **Measure** — Baseline what you've actually got before you touch anything. [Start with the 90-Minute Audit](/strategy/audit/).
2. **Fix** — Plug the technical and conversion leaks first. [What to fix, in order](/how-to-market-a-website/#step-2--fix-plug-the-leaks-before-you-buy-more-water).
3. **Strengthen** — Make the content worth ranking and worth citing. [The Content Audit for Live Sites](/content/audit/).
4. **Amplify** — Distribute a site that's finally worth distributing. [How distribution earns citations](/how-to-market-a-website/#step-4--amplify-distribution-for-a-site-thats-now-worth-it).

**Amplifying a leaky site is paying to lose faster.** So we measure and fix first. Always.

The whole method, laid out end to end and free: [How to Market a Website in 2026](/how-to-market-a-website/).

### 1.4 Where to Start

Start where the method starts. Not a newsletter popup. An audit.

Ninety minutes, and you'll know which of the four steps you're actually on.

[Run the 90-Minute Website Marketing Audit](/strategy/audit/)

# About site.marketing (and Sunny)

### 2.1 Why This Site Exists

site.marketing exists because every guide to website marketing assumes you launched yesterday. You didn't. You've got a live site, a few years of history, and a growth line that flattened out.

The whole "21 ways to promote your website" genre is written for a blank page — pick a channel, any channel, good luck. That advice is useless once you already have a hundred pages, some rankings, and a conversion leak you can't see. Nobody was writing for the site that already exists.

So I do. That's the gap, and this site owns it: the existing-site angle, treated as its own discipline with its own order of operations. One sequence, not a buffet.

### 2.2 Who Writes This

I'm Sunny Patel. I've spent 14 years in SEO, and these days I specialise in topical authority and entity SEO — the two things that decide whether Google and the answer engines actually trust your site.

I've deleted 400 pages from a client's site and watched the traffic climb. I've argued with founders who wanted to buy ads for a funnel that leaked at every seam. Most of what's on this site is a lesson I learned the expensive way, so you don't have to.

The 8% number you'll see everywhere here is mine. I ran the audits myself — 50 established sites, 20 buyer-language prompts each, across ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. Median citation share before any GEO work: 8%. Not a stat I borrowed. One I collected, and [the full method is public](/research/citation-share-study/).
